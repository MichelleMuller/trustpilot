# Competitive benchmark — the thing to beat

---

## Bazaarvoice Authentic Discovery API (April 2026)

The clearest statement of intent from any competitor, and the direct precedent for Bet 2.

**What it is:** an API-driven framework engineered for Generative Engine Optimisation, ensuring a brand's ratings, reviews and Q&A are *"structured and accessible for seamless indexing by AI agents and Large Language Models."*

**Key design choices worth copying or beating:**

| Choice | Detail | Read |
|---|---|---|
| **API, not a page** | Serves review content in a crawlable format for AI applications to access **directly** | They stopped relying on agents scraping PDPs. The lesson is the same for PRPs. |
| **Live, not batch** | *"Reflects current Bazaarvoice Ratings and Reviews data — the live source of truth — rather than a batch-processed export"* | **Freshness is the design principle**, and recency is one of the three factors Blair himself names |
| **Patent-pending** | Their **first patent-pending product since 2014** | They consider this strategically defensible, not a feature |
| **Enterprise-gated** | *"Limited to enterprise packages with specific display configurations, with wider availability planned"* | **Their weak point.** Narrow availability caps coverage, and coverage is what agents reward. |

**Their scale claim:** 2.3bn monthly shoppers in network, 20bn+ shopper touchpoints, plus syndication across thousands of retail sites producing *"the consistent consensus AI agents look for."*

**Their supporting stat, which is useful to us:** *"AI agents are 20–40% less likely to select products when key information is missing."*

---

## How to beat it

**Three openings, in order of strength:**

### 1. Openness beats gating
Bazaarvoice's API is enterprise-gated. Agents reward **coverage** — an API that covers a fraction of a fraction of products is a thin signal. Trustpilot's whole philosophy is *"make the data as useful as possible to as many people as possible."*

**An open agent surface, available across the whole catalogue rather than the enterprise tier, is strategically consistent *and* commercially differentiating.** It also pairs exactly with the free-widgets repackaging: stop monetising access, monetise visibility and insight.

### 2. Independence beats network
Bazaarvoice's content sits inside brand- and retailer-owned pages. It is first-party UGC displayed on the seller's own surface.

Trustpilot's content is **third-party by construction** — a business cannot delete a review, cannot remove its profile, cannot stop someone writing one. **That is the thing an agent is looking for when it reaches past brand-owned content**, and it is the one thing Bazaarvoice structurally cannot offer.

### 3. Provenance beats volume
Bazaarvoice leads on scale — 2.3bn shoppers, 20bn touchpoints. Trustpilot cannot win that argument and shouldn't try.

What Trustpilot can offer is **verifiable trust**: `isVerified`, `verificationLevel`, `reviewSource`, fraud models that removed 3.9m fakes in a half, a two-hour posting delay, weekly retraining. In an internet filling with synthetic review text, **the ability to tell an agent *how* a rating was verified is worth more than having more of them.**

## The others

**Yotpo** — positions its platform as feeding LLMs structured, high-quality data; AI Smart Prompts guide reviewers on what to write. Collection-side, not an agent-facing surface.

**Okendo, Judge.me, Loox** — strong collection and display for Shopify brands. No agent-facing infrastructure. These are the platforms whose content the inbound-syndication bet would aggregate.

**Profound, Peec, Scrunch (Sitecore)** — measure AI visibility; they don't supply the data agents consume. They can tell a brand it's invisible; they cannot make it visible. Trustpilot holds both ends.

---

## The summary

> Bazaarvoice got there first and patented it. But they built a **gated** API over **first-party** content and lead on **volume**. The counter is an **open** surface over **independent** content that leads on **provenance**. That's not a copy — it's the version Bazaarvoice structurally can't build.
