# Trustpilot Product Reviews — Strategy Assignment

Research base and working materials for a product strategy assignment:

> *In the world of AI, Trustpilot's Product Reviews offering is uniquely positioned to help consumers confidently evaluate products when making purchasing decisions and help businesses drive visibility of their products in LLMs.*
>
> **How would you evolve Product Reviews over the next 12–18 months to turn it into a high-impact product line for the business?**

Evaluated on three things:
1. **Strategic Direction** — where to place the biggest bets, and why
2. **Execution & Trade-offs** — the 2–3 concrete initiatives to prioritise first, and how to handle the trade-offs
3. **Impact & Metrics** — how to measure success and know the strategy is working
